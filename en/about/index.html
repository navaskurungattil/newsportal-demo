About Us

News Portal is a bilingual news website, in Malayalam and English, for Malayalis in the Gulf and in Kerala. We bring together news from Kerala, the Gulf, India and the world in one place, written to be clear and quick to read on a phone.

Our editorial principles

  • Accuracy first. We verify before we publish, and we name our sources wherever possible.
  • Fairness. We report all sides of a story and keep news separate from opinion.
  • Independence. Advertisers do not influence our news coverage. Advertisements are always clearly separated from news content.
  • Corrections. When we get something wrong, we correct it promptly and openly. Report an error to editor@newsportal.example.
