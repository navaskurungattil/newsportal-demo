Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

1. Who we are

News Portal is published by News Portal ("we", "us") at newsportal.example. For any privacy question, contact privacy@newsportal.example.

2. What we collect

You can read News Portal without an account. The public site has no comment section or forms.

  • Technical data: when you visit, our hosting and security providers automatically process your IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, referring website and time of visit. We use this to keep the site secure, prevent abuse and measure performance.
  • Advertising data: we use Google AdSense to show ads that fund our journalism. Google and its partners may use cookies and device identifiers to show and measure ads, including personalised ads where permitted. See how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Your display preference: your light/dark mode choice is stored only in your own browser and is never sent to us.
  • Emails you send us: if you email us, we keep your message and address to reply and to keep a record of corrections and requests.

3. Service providers

We share data only with providers that help run the site: our cloud hosting provider, Cloudflare (content delivery and security) and Google (advertising, and YouTube when you choose to play an embedded video; videos load from YouTube only after you click play). These providers may process data outside your country under their own safeguards.

4. Why we process data

To operate and secure the website, to understand and improve our service, and to show advertising. Where the law requires your consent, we ask for it, and you can withdraw it at any time.

5. How long we keep data

Server and security logs are kept for up to 90 days unless needed longer to investigate a security incident. Emails are kept for as long as needed to handle your request.

6. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw your consent at any time, and raise a complaint. You may also nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf. Email privacy@newsportal.example; we respond within 30 days. You can turn off personalised ads at Google Ad Settings.

7. Privacy contact

Data Protection Officer — privacy@newsportal.example

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the data protection authority in your country.

8. Children

News Portal is a general news service. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.

A Malayalam version of this policy is provided for convenience. If there is any difference, this English version applies.
